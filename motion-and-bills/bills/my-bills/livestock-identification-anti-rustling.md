### **ENACTED by the Plateau State House of Assembly as follows:**

#### **1. Short Title and Commencement**
(1) This Law may be cited as the **Plateau State Livestock Identification and Anti-Rustling Law, 2025**.
(2) It shall commence on **1st January 2026**.

#### **2. Interpretation**
In this Law:
> *"Database"* means the Central Livestock Database established under Section 4;
> *"Livestock"* means cattle, sheep, and goats kept or reared within Plateau State;
> *"Livestock Ownership Certificate"* means the document issued under Section 5 as prima facie proof of ownership;
> *"Ministry"* means the Plateau State Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development;
> *"Rustling"* means the theft, forcible taking, or unlawful driving away of livestock belonging to another person;
> *"Security Agency"* means the Nigeria Police Force, the Nigerian Army, and the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) operating within Plateau State;
> *"Ward Head"* and *"Ardo"* include any recognized traditional or community leader with authority over a locality.

#### **3. Objectives**
The objectives of this Law are to:
(a) Curb cattle rustling and reduce false ownership claims and illegal livestock trade;
(b) Provide a verifiable, biometric-backed system of livestock ownership;
(c) Protect citizens from arbitrary seizure of livestock and coercive "fish out thieves" practices that violate due process;
(d) Clarify the respective roles of security agencies and community leaders in livestock disputes;
(e) Reduce inter-communal tension arising from unresolved or wrongly resolved livestock disputes.

#### **4. Central Livestock Database**
(1) The Ministry shall establish and maintain a **Central Livestock Database** for Plateau State.
(2) The Database shall record, for each registered animal:
> (a) A biometric identifier (ear tag or microchip);
> (b) The verified owner's details, including National Identification Number (NIN);
> (c) The location and date of registration.
(3) The Ministry shall issue biometric identifiers for all cattle, sheep, and goats presented for registration.

#### **5. Livestock Ownership Certificates**
(1) Upon registration, the Ministry shall issue a **Livestock Ownership Certificate** to the verified owner.
(2) A Livestock Ownership Certificate shall constitute **prima facie proof of ownership** in any dispute, investigation, or proceeding concerning the animal to which it relates.

#### **6. Mandatory Registration**
(1) Every owner of livestock within Plateau State shall register such livestock with the Ministry within **twelve (12) months** of the commencement of this Law.
(2) Registration under this Law shall be free of charge.
(3) No person shall slaughter or sell livestock that has not been registered under this Law, except livestock brought into the State for immediate sale at a licensed abattoir or market, which shall be subject to registration procedures prescribed by regulation.

#### **7. Role of Security Agencies**
(1) Security Agencies shall have **exclusive responsibility** for the investigation of, and seizure of livestock in connection with, allegations of rustling or false ownership.
(2) Where livestock is seized on suspicion of rustling or false ownership, the Security Agency shall verify ownership against the Database within **forty-eight (48) hours** of seizure.
(3) Any dispute over ownership arising from a seizure shall be resolved within **seven (7) days** of the seizure.
(4) Livestock held pending verification or dispute resolution shall be kept in humane custody at the expense of the Fund established under Section 12.

#### **8. Role of Community and Traditional Leaders**
(1) Ward Heads, Ardos, and other community leaders may gather and pass on credible intelligence to Security Agencies regarding suspected rustling.
(2) Community and traditional leaders **shall not** conduct forced investigations, detain suspects, or otherwise act to "fish out thieves" outside the framework of this Law.
(3) A community or traditional leader who acts in contravention of subsection (2) commits an offence under Section 9.

#### **9. Offences and Penalties**
| **Offence** | **Penalty** |
|---|---|
| Rustling | Minimum of 20 years imprisonment, together with full restitution to the victim |
| False ownership claim over registered livestock | Minimum of 10 years imprisonment or a fine of not less than ₦5,000,000, or both |
| Tampering with, removing, or forging a biometric identifier or Livestock Ownership Certificate | 5 years imprisonment or a fine of ₦2,000,000, or both |
| Slaughter or sale of unregistered livestock in violation of Section 6(3) | ₦200,000 fine per animal |
| Forced investigation or coercive action by a community or traditional leader in violation of Section 8(2) | ₦500,000 fine or 2 years imprisonment, or both |

#### **10. Compensation for Unlawful Seizure**
(1) A livestock owner whose animals are unlawfully seized, or seized and not returned following verification of ownership under Section 7, shall be entitled to compensation from the Fund established under Section 12.
(2) Compensation shall cover the market value of the livestock and any provable consequential loss.

#### **11. Public Awareness**
The Ministry shall conduct statewide public awareness campaigns, in major local languages, on the registration system and the rights and obligations created by this Law, commencing not later than **three (3) months** after commencement of this Law.

#### **12. Funding**
(1) There is established a **Livestock Identification and Compensation Fund**, sourced from:
> (a) Budgetary allocation from the State Government;
> (b) Grants from federal or international development partners;
> (c) Fines recovered under Section 9.
(2) The Fund shall be applied to the operation of the Database, humane custody of disputed livestock under Section 7(4), and compensation under Section 10.

#### **13. Regulations**
The Commissioner for Agriculture and Rural Development may make regulations for:
(a) The registration procedure, including for livestock brought into the State for sale;
(b) The form of the Livestock Ownership Certificate;
(c) The operation and security of the Database;
(d) Any other matter necessary to give full effect to this Law.

#### **14. Savings Clause**
(1) Nothing in this Law shall be construed to derogate from the powers of Security Agencies under federal law.
(2) Livestock ownership disputes pending before a court or tribunal at the commencement of this Law shall continue to be determined under the law applicable before commencement.

### **Certification of Compliance**
This Law aligns with:
- **Constitution S.14(2)(b)**: Security and welfare of citizens as the primary purpose of government
- **S.17(2)(c)**: Protection against arbitrary exercise of power, including due process in seizures
- **S.36**: Right to fair hearing, reflected in the 48-hour verification and 7-day dispute resolution windows

### **Explanatory Note**
This Bill converts the House's motion "A Call to Establish a System for Mandatory Identification, Registration, and Ownership Verification of Livestock in Plateau State" into enforceable law. It gives Security Agencies exclusive, time-bound authority over rustling investigations and seizures, restricts community and traditional leaders to an intelligence-gathering role to prevent coercive "fish out thieves" practices, and creates a biometric registration system whose Ownership Certificates are usable as prima facie proof in disputes — reducing both rustling and the false-ownership claims that often follow it.

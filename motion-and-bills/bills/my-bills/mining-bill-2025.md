#### **A BILL FOR**
**A LAW TO ESTABLISH THE PLATEAU STATE MINERAL DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR THE SUSTAINABLE HARNESSING OF MINERAL RESOURCES IN PLATEAU STATE; TO PROVIDE FOR ENVIRONMENTAL SAFEGUARDS, HOST COMMUNITY BENEFITS AND REGULATION OF ARTISANAL MINING; AND FOR OTHER RELATED MATTERS**

### **ENACTED by the Plateau State House of Assembly as follows:**

#### **1. Short Title and Commencement**
(1) This Law may be cited as the **Plateau State Mineral Development Authority Law, 2025**.
(2) It shall commence on **1st January 2026**.

#### **2. Interpretation**
In this Law:
> *"Authority"* means the Plateau State Mineral Development Authority established under Section 3;
> *"Artisanal Miner"* means an individual or small-scale operator engaged in manual, non-mechanized mineral extraction;
> *"Board"* means the Governing Board of the Authority established under Section 5;
> *"EIA"* means an Environmental Impact Assessment conducted in accordance with Section 8;
> *"Host Community"* means any community within whose territory mining activities under this Law are conducted;
> *"Mineral Resources"* includes tin, columbite, limestone, gemstones, and other solid minerals located within Plateau State;
> *"Reclamation Bond"* means a financial guarantee deposited by a mining operator to secure post-mining site rehabilitation;
> *"Royalty"* means a payment made to the State by a mining operator calculated on the value or volume of minerals extracted.

#### **3. Establishment of the Authority**
(1) There is established a body to be known as the **Plateau State Mineral Development Authority**.
(2) The Authority shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, capable of suing and being sued in its corporate name.
(3) The Authority shall coordinate, within the limits of State competence, the sustainable development of mineral resources in Plateau State, without prejudice to the licensing powers vested in the Federal Government under the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act.

#### **4. Objectives of the Authority**
The Authority shall:
(a) Promote the sustainable and orderly development of Plateau State's mineral resources;
(b) Formalize and support artisanal and small-scale mining operators;
(c) Protect the environment and host communities from the adverse effects of mining activities;
(d) Ensure equitable distribution of mining-derived revenue between the State, host communities, and future development needs;
(e) Diversify the State's Internally Generated Revenue (IGR) through responsible mineral resource coordination.

#### **5. Governing Board**
(1) The Authority shall be governed by a Board comprising:
> (a) A Chairman appointed by the Governor;
> (b) A representative of the Ministry of Solid Minerals Development;
> (c) A representative of the Ministry of Environment;
> (d) Three (3) representatives of host communities, one from each senatorial zone;
> (e) Two (2) technical experts in geology or mining engineering;
> (f) A representative of registered artisanal miners' associations.
(2) Members shall hold office for a term of **four (4) years**, renewable once.
(3) The Board shall meet at least once every quarter.
(4) A member who has a direct or indirect pecuniary interest in any matter before the Board shall declare such interest and shall not vote on that matter.

#### **6. Functions and Powers of the Authority**
The Authority shall:
| **Function** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| Coordination | Coordinate mineral development activities within the State, consistent with federal law |
| Registration | Maintain the State Mineral Database and register artisanal miners |
| Environmental Oversight | Enforce EIA and reclamation bond requirements |
| Revenue Management | Collect and administer royalties and the Host Community Development Fund |
| Advisory | Advise the State Government on mineral policy and investment promotion |
| Reporting | Submit quarterly reports to the State House of Assembly |

#### **7. State Mineral Database and Artisanal Miner Registration**
(1) The Authority shall establish and maintain a **State Mineral Database**, being a digital registry of known mineral deposits and mining activity within the State.
(2) Every artisanal miner operating within Plateau State shall register with the Authority within **twelve (12) months** of the commencement of this Law.
(3) Registration shall be free of charge for artisanal miners and shall entitle the registrant to:
> (a) Technical support and safety training;
> (b) Access to formal mineral markets;
> (c) Inclusion in the Host Community Development Fund distribution framework where applicable.

#### **8. Environmental Safeguards**
(1) No mining project shall commence within Plateau State without an approved **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)**.
(2) Every mining operator shall deposit a **Reclamation Bond** with the Authority prior to commencement of operations, the amount to be determined by regulation.
(3) The Reclamation Bond shall be forfeited, in whole or in part, where an operator fails to rehabilitate a mined site in accordance with the approved EIA.
(4) The Authority shall inspect active mining sites not less than twice a year to verify compliance.

#### **9. Host Community Development Fund**
(1) There is established a **Host Community Development Fund**, into which shall be paid **ten percent (10%)** of all mineral royalties collected under this Law.
(2) The Fund shall be applied exclusively to development projects within the host community from which the underlying royalty was derived, including but not limited to water, health, education, and road infrastructure.
(3) A Host Community Development Committee, comprising community representatives, shall determine priority projects for Fund disbursement, subject to the Board's approval.
(4) The Authority shall publish an annual statement of Fund disbursements by community.

#### **10. Funding of the Authority**
(1) The Authority shall be funded from:
> (a) Mineral royalties collected under this Law;
> (b) Budgetary allocations from the State Government;
> (c) Grants, donations, and technical assistance from federal or international development partners.
(2) The Authority shall not expend Fund resources designated under Section 9 for its own administrative costs.

#### **11. Reporting and Audit**
(1) The Authority shall submit **quarterly reports** of its activities, revenue collections, and Fund disbursements to the State House of Assembly under Section 128(1)(b) of the Constitution.
(2) The accounts of the Authority shall be audited annually by the Office of the State Auditor-General, and the audit report shall be made available to the public.

#### **12. Offences and Penalties**
| **Offence** | **Penalty** |
|---|---|
| Mining without registration or required approvals ("illegal mining") | ₦5,000,000 fine or 5 years imprisonment, or both |
| Causing environmental damage in violation of an approved EIA | Full restitution plus ₦2,000,000 fine |
| Misuse or diversion of Host Community Development Fund monies by a Board member or officer | Criminal liability, removal from office, and refund of the misappropriated sum |
| Failure to register as an artisanal miner after the compliance period | ₦50,000 fine |

#### **13. Regulations**
The Commissioner for Solid Minerals Development may, on the recommendation of the Board, make regulations for:
(a) The determination and review of royalty rates and reclamation bond amounts;
(b) The registration procedure for artisanal miners;
(c) The composition and operation of the Host Community Development Committees;
(d) Any other matter necessary for giving full effect to this Law.

#### **14. Repeal and Savings**
(1) The **Plateau State Minerals Edict, 1983**, is hereby repealed.
(2) Any mining license, permit, or right validly issued or subsisting before the commencement of this Law shall continue in force as if issued under this Law, until its expiry or lawful revocation.
(3) This Law shall not be construed to confer on the State any power to grant or revoke mineral licenses, that power remaining vested in the Federal Government under the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act and Item 39 of the Exclusive Legislative List; the Authority's functions under this Law are limited to coordination, registration, environmental oversight, and revenue-sharing within the State.

### **Certification of Compliance**
This Law aligns with:
- **Constitution S.16(1)(a) & (2)(d)**: State role in economic coordination of natural resources
- **S.17(3)**: Social justice and equitable distribution of resource benefits
- **S.20**: Protection and improvement of the environment
- **S.128(1)(b)**: Legislative oversight through quarterly reporting
- **Exclusive Legislative List, Item 39**: Recognizes federal primacy over mineral licensing; the Authority's role is confined to coordination, not licensing

### **Explanatory Note**
This Bill establishes a Plateau State Mineral Development Authority to coordinate the sustainable development of the State's mineral resources, formalize the State's estimated 20,000+ artisanal miners, and ensure host communities receive a direct, transparent share of mining revenue. It is designed to operate strictly within the State's constitutional competence, leaving federal licensing powers undisturbed while giving Plateau State the institutional capacity to benefit from, and safeguard against the harms of, mineral extraction within its territory.
